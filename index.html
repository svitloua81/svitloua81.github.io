Know when the lights go off — and when they come back.

Everything about your power, in one place

Built around the schedules your regional operator publishes — so you can plan the day instead of guessing.

🏠

Several addresses

Home, work, parents — each address with its own region and outage group. The card shows at a glance whether the power is on, until when, and how long is left.

📅

Today & tomorrow

A day timeline with every outage interval, viewable by periods or hour by hour, plus a summary of hours with and without power.

🧩

Home-screen widgets

Two sizes: a 4×2 widget with the current state, next event and day bar, and a compact one-line 4×1. Both update on their own.

🔔

Notifications

A heads-up a few minutes before the power goes off or comes back — you choose how early — and an alert when the operator publishes a new schedule.

🗺️

12 regions of Ukraine

Cherkasy, Chernihiv, Kharkiv, Khmelnytskyi, Lviv, Poltava, Ivano-Frankivsk, Rivne, Ternopil, Zakarpattia, Zaporizhzhia, Zhytomyr. New regions appear automatically.

📴

Works offline

The last schedule is kept on your device, so you can check it even without an internet connection. Available in Ukrainian, English and Russian.
